## ✅ Trademarked style names — RESOLVED in this build

Item 7 ("protected third-party material") is partly probing trademarked brand
names. The user-facing style names and the App Store keywords/description have
been renamed to remove them (palettes unchanged):
- "Game Boy" → **Handheld '89**
- "Game Boy Pocket" → **Pocket '96**
- "PICO-8" → **Fantasy 16**
- "Commodore 64" → **Home '82**
- Untouched (generic/community, no exposure): 1-Bit Ink, Green CRT, Amber CRT,
  Sepia, CGA, Sweetie 16, Modern Clean.

Store keywords no longer contain `gameboy`/`pico8` (or the KO/JA equivalents);
they now use generic terms (handheld, arcade). Internal source identifiers
(`gameboy`, `pico8`, `c64`) are unchanged — they are not shipped or user-visible
and are needed for the free-tier/StoreKit matching to keep working.
